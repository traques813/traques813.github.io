## About

Grown from quiet places where tradition meets solitude, their voice reflects on the weight of heritage, the darkness we carry, and the fragile ways knowledge passes from mouth to mouth. Drawing from rural memory, faith, and lived experience, their work explores the tension between knowing the land and struggling to understand the wider world — finding meaning in shared proverbs, unspoken rituals, and the stubborn dignity of small lives searching for light.
